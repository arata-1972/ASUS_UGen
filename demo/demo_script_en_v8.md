# TaigiCareNote v8 — English narrated demo

126.52 seconds. Edited screenshots from actual local review operations; not continuous screen recording. Fictional care text, deliberate draft discrepancies, no audio API calls and no UGen inference. English narration: macOS Samantha.

## Scene 1 | 0–14.84 seconds

TaigiCareNote — review before handover

A care worker needs a record that the next shift can trust. TaigiCareNote helps compare a draft with its source before handover. This demonstration runs the new review workflow on fictional text. Audio and speech recognition are not executed.

## Scene 2 | 14.84–30.68 seconds

Keep the original sentence beside the draft

The fictional source says: at nine this morning, the person receiving care reported left knee pain. No dizziness. Two hundred milliliters of water. Each draft statement refers to an exact source position. The rule-based template does not invent a medical summary.

## Scene 3 | 30.68–45.72 seconds

Controlled differences: side and quantity

For this controlled test, we intentionally change left to right and two hundred to two hundred fifty. The screen flags side, negation, and quantity for a person to review. These are test defects, not measured speech recognition errors.

## Scene 4 | 45.72–57.6 seconds

Correct the draft and record the checks

The reviewer compares the source, corrects left and two hundred, and writes a note for each check. No dizziness stays in the record. The software does not infer a diagnosis or recommend treatment.

## Scene 5 | 57.6–71.48 seconds

Approve this exact version

Approval ties the reviewer and time to the content version and fingerprints of the source, draft, and review decisions. JSON and CSV are unlocked only after the checks. Unsaved edits also lock approval and export.

## Scene 6 | 71.48–91.48 seconds

Export the approved record and its history

The actual JSON and CSV exports carry the same approved version, source evidence, review notes, and events. Editing afterwards creates a new version and locks export again. The existing medical PoC already had review and confirmation. New work links approval and export to content hashes and detailed events.

## Scene 7 | 91.48–107.36 seconds

Stage II: validate ASR in the designated environment

The planned next step is speech recognition on UGen B70 in the designated competition environment. Review checks remain on the host. Hardware compatibility and performance are not demonstrated. The new review logic does not require a large language model.

## Scene 8 | 107.36–126.52 seconds

A handover that people can review and trace

Seventeen local tests pass for review gates, content checks, and export consistency. These are workflow tests, not clinical validation. Next, we will evaluate review time and corrections with permitted fictional or synthetic cases. The goal is a record a person has checked and a colleague can trace.
